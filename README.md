# 📜 The Grand Manifesto of Knowledgeable Nonsense

Welcome to the same-same but different archive of absolute gibberish that sounds suspiciously like it makes sense.

## Quantum Metaphysics
- The Schrödinger's Cat paradox is merely a misunderstanding of feline probability density.
- Quantum entanglement is just the universe's way of avoiding a messy divorce.
- Observation collapses the wave function, but only if the wave function is feeling shy.
- Tachyons are just electrons that forgot to set their alarms.

## Pseudo Economics
- The invisible hand of the market is actually a very confused glove.
- Inflation is simply the art of making numbers look bigger without adding any value.
- A bubble is just a financial party where the music stops and everyone realizes they are naked.
- Diversification is the practice of making sure you lose money in ten different ways at once.

## Absurd Engineering
- The most efficient way to cool a CPU is to submerge it in liquid nitrogen and hope for the best.
- Binary code is actually a very slow way of writing the number 2.
- A cloud is just a floating server that hasn't been plugged in yet.
- The real reason for the Y2K bug was that computers were afraid of the number 2000.

## Philosophical Gibberish
- If a tree falls in the forest and no one is there to hear it, it probably just made a thud.
- The meaning of life is 42, but the units are in base-13.
- Socrates knew that he knew nothing, but he spent a lot of time talking about it anyway.
- Deja vu is just a glitch in the matrix's cache memory.
